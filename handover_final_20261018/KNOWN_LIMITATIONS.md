# Known Limitations — Smart Pesticide Residue Detection System
**Version:** Candidate (2026-10-18)  
**Required reading before any demonstration or assessment**

---

## LIMITATION 1 — Apple and Guava Classification

**Status: UNRESOLVED — Requires new hardware training data**

### What happens
- Physical Apple placed in front of camera: firmware correctly **rejects** (NOFRUIT/UNKNOWN).  
  Raw classifier output: Guava, 80.1% confidence (below per-class threshold for Guava: 97.6%).
- Physical Guava placed in front of camera: firmware correctly **rejects** (NOFRUIT/UNKNOWN).  
  Raw classifier output: Apple, 60.9% confidence (below per-class threshold for Apple: 99.2%).

### Why it happens
1. **Domain gap:** All Apple and Guava training images come from FruitNet (smartphone captures, uniform white backgrounds). The OV2640 camera on the STM32 board uses a different colour space, compression artifacts, and real-world lighting.
2. **Quantified baseline failure:** On the clean public test set, 83.6% of Apple images already fail the 0.976 confidence gate — even before the OV2640 domain shift.
3. **Head-only retraining failed:** Experiment `E2` showed that retraining only the final classifier layers improves Guava recall but collapses Apple recall from 0.727 to 0.625. Full backbone fine-tuning is required.

### What is needed to fix it
- Minimum 30–50 OV2640 captures per class (Apple, Guava) under representative lighting and backgrounds.
- Per-class confidence calibration on OV2640 images.
- Full backbone fine-tuning (not head-only) against OV2640 training data.

### What the system does correctly
- The **presence head** and **confidence gate** correctly prevent misclassified Apple/Guava frames from appearing on the OLED. The user sees `Fruit:NOFRUIT/UNKNOWN`, not a wrong fruit name.
- This is conservative and honest — the system does not fabricate a result.

## LIMITATION 2 — Pesticide Risk Classification (Model 2)

**Status: SYNTHETIC DATA ONLY — Not validated on real produce**

- The risk classifier (`model2_prototype`) was trained on **synthetic data generated by a rule-based script**, not on laboratory-verified pesticide measurements.
- All risk outputs on the OLED (`Risk:UNCERTAIN`) reflect this: the system correctly marks risk as uncertain because no real reference measurements exist.
- **Do not claim the system can detect pesticide residues.** It cannot. The sensor suite (pH, MQ135, TCS34725) has not been calibrated against any accredited analytical standard.

### Required to make risk classification valid
1. Calibrated sensor profiles (see `docs/SENSOR_CALIBRATION_PROTOCOL.md`).
2. Reference-labelled samples: LC-MS/MS or GC-MS analysis on the same produce lot.
3. Model retrained on real calibrated sensor readings against verified risk bands.

## LIMITATION 3 — Sensor Calibration

**Status: UNCALIBRATED — Readings are indicative only**

| Sensor | Current Status | Required |
|--------|---------------|---------|
| pH (ENTC probe) | Reads 9.28 — outside valid fruit range (3–7); not calibrated against buffers | 3-point calibration with certified buffers (4.01, 6.86, 9.18) |
| MQ135 | ADC reading only; R0 not established; warm-up flag present | 24 h burn-in, R0 in clean air, Rs/R0 normalization |
| TCS34725 | Raw counts; no white-reference normalization | Fixed-ATIME/AGAIN, white and dark reference per session |
| DHT22 | Not checked against reference thermometer | Spot-check against reference |

## LIMITATION 4 — Natural-Growth Score

**Status: NOT IMPLEMENTED**

The concept of a "natural growth score" does not exist in the firmware or models. It must not be displayed or claimed. Any reference to natural growth in earlier documentation describes a future possibility, not a current feature.

## LIMITATION 6 — OLED Footer Source vs. Flashed Firmware

**Status: Source fixed; board has pre-fix binary**

The source code (`firmware/stm32f407/app/fv_report.c`) no longer emits `*proto, not lab test` on OLED row 8.  
The currently flashed binary pre-dates this source change. The physical OLED still shows the footer.  
A reflash is required to remove it from the physical display. The corrected binary is included in this package.

## LIMITATION 7 — Vision Arena Used vs. Persistent Arena Size

**Status: Documented, not a bug**

- `vision_arena_used` at runtime: 149,252 bytes (in non-persistent SRAM1 arena: 97,280 B allocated + 54,272 B persistent CCMRAM).
- The TFLM allocator uses both arenas sequentially. All checks pass in the build report.
- The `ERR_MODEL = -5` allocation failure documented in earlier reports was resolved by setting `FV_ARENA_PERSISTENT_BYTES = 54272` (increased from 53248). This change is in the candidate firmware.
